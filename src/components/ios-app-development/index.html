BEFORE YOU HIRE

Your iOS questions.
Clear answers.

Start with the essentials, then work through the details of your own project.

Talk about your project →
Can I hire iOS developers for an existing app?

Yes. Share your current app, codebase, and priorities so the team can assess the work needed for new features, interface improvements, or ongoing development.

Should my app use SwiftUI or UIKit?

The choice depends on your existing codebase, supported devices, interface needs, and roadmap. SwiftUI and UIKit can also work together. The technical approach should be agreed during discovery.

Can the same project support iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Define the devices and key use cases upfront so layouts, interactions, and testing account for both phone and tablet experiences.

Will you help with TestFlight and App Store preparation?

Release preparation can be included in the scope, including beta builds, feedback, submission materials, and handoff. Your business should retain ownership of its developer account.

How much does it cost to hire iOS developers?

Cost depends on features, integrations, the existing codebase, team needs, and project duration. Share your requirements to receive a project-specific estimate rather than a generic rate.

Who owns the code and design files?

You own the code, assets, and design files. Define ownership and handoff terms in your project agreement.
