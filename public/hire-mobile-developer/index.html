Hiring questions

Straight answers before you commit. Anything else, ask us in chat.

How quickly can a developer start?

It depends on the skills and seniority you need. Once we have your requirements we share a shortlist, and developers can start as soon as you choose them.

Can I interview the developers?

Yes. You interview every candidate and decide who joins your team.

Will the developers work in my time zone?

We arrange working-hour overlap with your team so stand-ups, reviews and questions happen in real time.

Who owns the code?

You do. Developers sign NDAs and all code, designs and IP belong to your company.

Can I scale the team up or down?

Yes. You can add developers, designers or QA as your roadmap grows, or reduce the team when a phase ends.

What if a developer is not the right fit?

Tell your account manager. We will work with you to resolve it or propose another candidate.
