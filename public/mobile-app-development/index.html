Mobile app development questions

Straight answers before you commit. Anything else, ask us in chat.

How long does it take to build a mobile app?

Most projects take 12 to 20 weeks including planning, design, development and testing. Agile sprints and parallel workflows help us deliver faster where possible.

How much does a mobile app cost?

It depends on features, platforms and integrations. After a free consultation we give you a detailed estimate with a fixed scope or a dedicated team option.

Should I build native or cross-platform?

Native gives the best performance and access to every device feature. Cross-platform gets you to both stores faster from one codebase. We recommend based on your product, not our preference.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We protect your intellectual property with strict NDAs, secure data handling and compliance protocols from kickoff to launch.

Who owns the source code?

You do. The code, designs and store listings are yours once the project is delivered.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We provide maintenance, bug fixes, performance optimization and OS updates so your app stays fast and secure.
