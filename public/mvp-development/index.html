The MVP mistakes we help you avoid

Most MVPs fail for reasons that have nothing to do with code. We build guardrails into the process.

  • Building too much. Every extra feature delays launch and blurs what you are testing.
  • No success metric. Without a clear goal, you cannot tell if the MVP worked.
  • Throwaway code. A rushed build that has to be rewritten the moment it gains traction.
  • Skipping user testing. Launching without testing the flow with real people first.
  • No analytics. Users arrive, but you have no data on what they do.
  • No plan for version two. Momentum stalls after launch without a roadmap.

MVP development questions

Straight answers before you commit. Anything else, ask us in chat.

How long does it take to build an MVP?

Most MVPs launch in 8 to 16 weeks depending on scope. A clickable prototype can be ready much sooner.

How much does an MVP cost?

It depends on features and platforms. After the scoping call we give you a fixed-scope estimate so there are no surprises.

Will I have to rebuild the MVP later?

No. We build on a clean, scalable architecture so your MVP becomes the foundation for version two, not a throwaway.

Can you help me decide what to build first?

Yes. Scoping is the most important part of an MVP. We run a discovery workshop to prioritize features against your success metric.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We sign an NDA before you share your idea, and you own all code and designs.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. We offer iteration sprints, analytics reviews and a dedicated team to take the product to the next stage.
