From build file to store listing in six steps

A typical first release takes one to three weeks, depending on account verification and store review times.

  1. 1

    Publishing audit

    We test your build against current App Store Review Guidelines and Google Play policies and list everything that could trigger a rejection.

  2. 2

    Accounts and access

    We create or verify your developer accounts in your company's name, so you always own the app and its listing.

  3. 3

    Store assets

    Listing copy, keywords, screenshots, icon and feature graphic are prepared and sent to you for approval.

  4. 4

    Compliance

    Privacy policy, data disclosures, content rating, demo login for reviewers and any required in-app changes.

  5. 5

    Submission and review

    We sign and upload the build, submit for review and answer reviewer questions until the app is approved.

  6. 6

    Launch and monitor

    We release on the date you choose, watch crash and review data, and prepare the first update if needed.

Already rejected? We fix these every week.

Send us the rejection message. We will tell you exactly what triggered it and what it takes to get approved.

  • Guideline 2.1 App completeness: crashes, broken links or no working demo account for reviewers.
  • Guideline 4.3 Spam: the app looks too similar to other apps or to a template.
  • Guideline 5.1.1 Data collection: missing privacy policy, unclear permission prompts or no account deletion.
  • Guideline 3.1.1 In-app purchase: digital goods sold outside Apple's payment system.
  • Play Data safety Declared data practices do not match what the app or its SDKs actually collect.
  • Play target API level The app does not target a recent enough Android version to be published or updated.

Questions about publishing

Straight answers on accounts, review times and ownership. Anything else, ask us in chat.

How long does it take to publish an app?

Most first releases go live within one to three weeks. Account verification can take a few days, Apple review usually takes 24 to 48 hours, and Google Play review ranges from a few hours to about a week. New personal Google Play accounts also need a closed test period before production access.

Who owns the developer account and the app?

You do. We create or configure accounts in your name or your company's name and work as a team member with limited access. The app, its listing, its reviews and its revenue stay yours.

What do I need to provide?

Your app build or source code, your business details for account verification, and your logo and brand guidelines. We prepare the rest and send everything for your approval before submission.

Can you publish an app that another company built?

Yes. We start with an audit of the build. If anything would cause a rejection, we tell you what it is and can fix it for you or brief your developers.

What happens if my app gets rejected?

We handle it. We read the reviewer's notes, make the required changes, reply in Resolution Center and resubmit until the app is approved.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We protect your intellectual property with NDAs and secure data handling from the first call to launch day.

Do you help after the app is live?

Yes. We manage updates, staged rollouts, ratings and reviews, and store optimization so your app keeps growing after launch.
