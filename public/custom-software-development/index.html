Why build custom software?

Off-the-shelf software makes you adapt to it. Custom software adapts to you and becomes an advantage competitors can’t buy.

  • Fits your workflow. Built around how your team actually works.
  • No per-seat licensing. You own it, so costs don’t grow with every new user.
  • Connects everything. One source of truth across your tools and data.
  • Scales with you. Architecture designed for your growth, not a vendor’s roadmap.
  • Secure by design. Access control, encryption and compliance built in.

Custom software questions

Straight answers before you commit. Anything else, ask us in chat.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on scope, integrations and complexity. After discovery we provide a detailed estimate, either as a fixed price or a dedicated team.

How long does it take?

Smaller tools can launch in a few weeks. Larger platforms are delivered in phases, with a first release typically in 12 to 20 weeks.

Who owns the software?

You do. All source code, designs and documentation belong to your company.

Can you integrate with our existing systems?

Yes. We regularly integrate with CRMs, ERPs, payment gateways, accounting tools and custom APIs.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. We sign NDAs and follow secure data handling from the first conversation.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, monitoring, security updates and ongoing feature development.
